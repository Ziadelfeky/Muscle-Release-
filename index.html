05Disclaimer & Consent
What to Expect During the Session

Muscle Release & Recovery involves hands-on manual therapy including soft tissue work, myofascial release, and targeted pressure techniques performed by Coach Ziad Elfeky. All work is conducted professionally and with complete respect for personal boundaries at all times.

Post-Session Effects

In most cases, clients feel immediate relief, lightness, and improved movement after the session. However, the following may occur — particularly in individuals with sensitive skin or areas of dense tension:

  • Temporary muscle soreness lasting 24–48 hours — similar to post-exercise soreness
  • Mild bruising in treated areas — this is uncommon but possible, especially with deep tissue work or sensitive skin. It is a normal physiological response, not a sign of injury, and typically resolves within 24–48 hours
  • Temporary redness in the treated area — normal and expected
Oil & Skin

For skin sessions, a professional massage oil will be applied to the treated areas. Please ensure you have disclosed any known skin sensitivities, allergies, or skin conditions in the Health section above. If you experience unexpected irritation during the session, inform your coach immediately and the session will be adjusted.

Your Right to Stop

You may stop the session at any time, for any reason, without explanation. You are encouraged to communicate any discomfort, boundaries, or preferences before and during the session — they will be respected without question.
